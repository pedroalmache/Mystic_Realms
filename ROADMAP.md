# Mystic Realms — Development Roadmap

## Overview

Mystic Realms is an actively developed game with several core systems, gameplay mechanics, and content features still planned for implementation.

This roadmap outlines upcoming development work, organized into milestones. The goal is to build a complete, balanced, and engaging gameplay experience with meaningful character progression, challenging encounters, and rewarding exploration.

Features and priorities may evolve as development progresses.

## Phase 1 — Rooms & Exploration

**Goal:** Expand the variety of rooms players encounter and introduce meaningful decisions during exploration.

- [ ] **Rewards Room**
  - Implement a room where players select from available rewards.
  - Design reward selection and distribution mechanics.
  - Integrate rewards with character progression and inventory systems.

- [ ] **Healing Room**
  - Implement rooms where players can recover health.
  - Define healing limitations and balancing rules.
  - Add visual feedback for healing interactions.

- [ ] **Shop Room**
  - Implement a shop interface for purchasing items and upgrades.
  - Create item pricing and purchase mechanics.
  - Integrate shops with the game's currency system.

- [ ] **Treasure Room**
  - Implement treasure encounters with randomized rewards.
  - Define treasure rarity and loot tables.
  - Add treasure discovery and collection interactions.

## Phase 2 — Enemies & Combat Encounters

**Goal:** Expand combat variety and introduce increasingly challenging encounters.

- [ ] **Additional Enemies**
  - Create new enemy types with distinct abilities and combat behaviors.
  - Introduce enemy difficulty tiers.
  - Balance enemy statistics, attack patterns, and rewards.

- [ ] **Boss Rooms**
  - Implement dedicated boss encounters.
  - Create unique boss mechanics and attack patterns.
  - Introduce boss-specific rewards and progression requirements.

- [ ] **Final Boss**
  - Design and implement a final boss encounter.
  - Create multiple attack patterns and combat phases.
  - Define victory conditions and endgame rewards.

## Phase 3 — Characters & Deck Building

**Goal:** Give players more control over their characters and combat strategies.

- [ ] **Character Decks**
  - Implement individual decks for playable characters.
  - Create systems for deck creation, editing, and management.
  - Define character-specific cards and abilities.
  - Introduce deck progression and balancing.

- [ ] **Character Customization**
  - Implement character customization options.
  - Introduce configurable character attributes or abilities.
  - Integrate customization with character progression.
  - Persist customization choices between gameplay sessions.

## Phase 4 — Progression & Scoring

**Goal:** Create a progression system that rewards continued play and increasing mastery.

- [ ] **Levels**
  - Implement character or gameplay leveling mechanics.
  - Define experience requirements and progression curves.
  - Unlock new challenges or abilities through progression.

- [ ] **Points**
  - Implement a points and scoring system.
  - Award points for combat, exploration, and accomplishments.
  - Display scores during gameplay and in end-of-run summaries.

- [ ] **Ascended Mechanic**
  - Design and implement the Ascended system.
  - Define conditions for activating or unlocking Ascended states.
  - Integrate Ascended effects into combat and progression.
  - Balance bonuses, limitations, and gameplay interactions.

- [ ] **Agony Mechanic**
  - Design and implement the Agony system.
  - Define how Agony is accumulated, triggered, or applied.
  - Implement its effects on character abilities and combat.
  - Balance risk, rewards, and interactions with other mechanics.

## Phase 5 — Audio & Gameplay Polish

**Goal:** Improve game feel, feedback, and overall presentation.

- [ ] **Sound Effects**
  - Add sound effects for attacks, abilities, and damage.
  - Add feedback sounds for rewards, purchases, and healing.
  - Introduce audio cues for enemies and boss encounters.
  - Add audio settings and volume controls.

- [ ] **Gameplay Balancing**
  - Balance character decks and abilities.
  - Adjust enemy difficulty and boss encounters.
  - Refine reward distribution and progression pacing.

- [ ] **Testing & Stability**
  - Add automated tests for core gameplay systems.
  - Fix gameplay and interface bugs.
  - Improve performance and reliability.
  - Test interactions between the different gameplay mechanics.
